SCRUNCH INTELLIGENCE - READ ME FIRST

1. Double-click "1 Set up Scrunch (once)". It installs everything: Mixing, the Scrunch HUD over Live,
   Audio-to-MIDI and Hum-to-MIDI, and the Scrunch Bridge for Ableton Live. Allow 15-40 minutes (the
   Audio-to-MIDI model is a 5.5 GB download; setup tells you how to get the free Hugging Face token it needs).
2. At the end it opens START SCRUNCH and puts it in your Dock (orange Scrunch logo, the word START).
   That is your start button: click it any time to open Scrunch in your web browser.
   Closed the browser? Click Start Scrunch again and the page comes back.
3. The first time, Scrunch opens its Settings page: add your engineer (AI) key and voice there.
4. In Ableton Live, once: quit Live and open it again, then Live > Settings > Link, Tempo & MIDI,
   choose ScrunchBridge in an empty Control Surface slot (Input and Output: None).

The first time you open each one, macOS may say it is from an unidentified developer.
Right-click (or Control-click) it, choose Open, then Open again. You only do this once.
The HUD asks for Accessibility (to follow the Live window) and Microphone (hold space to talk) the first time.

Keep this folder where it is (Documents is fine): Start Scrunch runs the copy inside it.
To remove everything: double-click "Uninstall Scrunch" and type yes. Your music and Sets are never touched.
